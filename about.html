Geography & Nature

Nepal is a landlocked nation located in South Asia, nestled between China to the north and India to the south. Divided into three major geographical regions—the Himalayan Region, the Hilly Region, and the Terai Plains—it hosts incredible biodiversity ranging from sub-tropical jungles to arctic alpine environments.

Culture & Heritage

Nepal is a rich tapestry of ethnic groups, languages, and religions. Hinduism and Buddhism have coexisted peacefully here for centuries. Lumbini, located in southern Nepal, is celebrated as the birthplace of Lord Gautama Buddha.

Adventure Tourism

Known as a paradise for trekkers and mountain climbers, Nepal attracts thousands of adventurers every year seeking to conquer Mount Everest or trek through popular circuits like Annapurna and Langtang.
